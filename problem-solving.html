DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Business Context

My Role: Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

As Chief Technology Officer, my mission is to lead the digital transformation of Sizzle & Sear by developing a proposed web solution that improves customer engagement, enhances operational efficiency and delivers a better customer experience.

The goal is to provide customers with a user-friendly website featuring online menus, reservation bookings and customer feedback functionality.

Problem Context

Many restaurants face challenges keeping up with technological advancements and continue to rely on traditional methods of operation.

However, customers increasingly prefer convenient digital services, such as viewing restaurant menus online and making reservations before visiting. Without these features, restaurants may struggle to meet changing customer expectations and remain competitive.

Proposed Solution

01

Online Menu

Allow customers to browse available dishes, descriptions and prices before visiting.

02

Online Booking Reservation System

Enable customers to submit reservation requests through a convenient online form.

03

Customer Feedback

Provide customers with an accessible way to share dining experiences, suggestions and feedback.

04

User-Friendly & Accessible Design

Create a clear, responsive website that is easy to navigate on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Notice

Sizzle & Sear is a fictional restaurant created for a university digital transformation case study. This demonstration website does not use a database to store reservation or feedback form submissions.

Information entered into the forms is used locally in your browser to prepare an email draft. The email is not sent automatically. You can review or cancel the message in your email application.

GDPR Considerations

The proposed solution considers data minimisation, transparency and user control. Customers should only be asked for information relevant to their reservation or feedback request.

Cookie Consent

This demonstration does not introduce advertising or analytics tracking cookies. A cookie information reminder is provided to explain how the page works.

This page does not set tracking cookies. Form details are not saved by this page. The separate portfolio cookie preference feature may remember a choice using your browser's local storage.
