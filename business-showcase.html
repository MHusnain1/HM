GROUP PRESENTATION

Student Mental Health Problems

📋

Project Overview

A university group presentation exploring student mental health challenges, raising awareness and proposing a practical solution to support student wellbeing.

⚠️

Problem Addressed

A lack of awareness surrounding student mental health, including difficulties recognising mental health problems at an early stage or identifying them at all.

📊

My Role & Responsibilities

I was responsible for developing a dashboard and creating charts to visually highlight the impact and high rates of student mental health problems. These visualisations supported our group's research and findings.

💡

Solution & Approach

Our group proposed a health and wellbeing app as a potential solution to improve mental health awareness and help students access wellbeing support.

🎯

Project Outcome

We successfully delivered a coordinated group presentation that communicated the importance of student mental health, presented supporting data and introduced our proposed health and wellbeing app.

03
BUSINESS CASE STUDY

Restaurant Digital Transformation

🍽️

Project Overview

A business case study exploring how a restaurant could use digital transformation to strengthen its online presence, modernise operations and improve the customer experience.

⚠️

Problem Addressed

The restaurant needed to improve its digital presence and modernise its operations to better serve customers while remaining competitive in the restaurant industry.

💻

My Role & Responsibilities

I took on the role of Chief Technology Officer (CTO), focusing on the technological aspects of the restaurant's digital transformation and how technology could support its business objectives.

💡

Solution & Approach

The case study focused on exploring digital improvements that could strengthen the restaurant's online presence, support more efficient operations and improve customer service.

🎯

Design Concept

My design concept for Sizzle & Sear was to create a modern, professional and user-friendly steakhouse website that reflects the restaurant's identity while improving the overall customer experience. I chose a dark colour scheme with gold accents to create a premium restaurant atmosphere, using clear headings, organised sections and attractive visuals to make the website engaging and easy to navigate.

The website features an animated homepage banner, an interactive food menu, a weekend promotional offer and a reservation form. I also included opening hours, customer feedback and contact information to help visitors find the information they need. These features were designed to make the website more convenient for customers while supporting the restaurant's marketing and customer engagement.

From a technology perspective, I used HTML, CSS and JavaScript to develop a responsive and interactive website. As the project's Chief Technology Officer, my aim was to demonstrate how digital solutions could strengthen a restaurant's online presence, promote its services and support its wider business objectives.

Relevant Skills & Approaches
